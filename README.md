# Lokebox case study

Lokebox is a platform for business automation, system integrations and internal tools.

It started from a practical need: finding a better way to handle operational and administrative work that was spread across spreadsheets, messages and disconnected tools.

Over time, those internal solutions evolved into a reusable platform for working with different kinds of business operations.

## What it helps solve

Lokebox is useful when a company has work that is too manual, information that lives in different places, or existing software that does not fully cover the way the business actually operates.

Typical use cases include:

- Registering operational and financial activity
- Connecting data from different systems
- Customer and supplier account tracking
- Dashboards and reporting
- Document processing
- Messaging-based workflows
- Internal tools built around a specific operation

## How I approach these projects

I usually start by understanding how the work is done today.

That means looking at who does what, where the information comes from, which tools are already in use, and where the friction is.

From there, the solution might be an automation, an integration, an internal dashboard, a custom application, or a combination of several things.

I do not assume that every problem needs a new platform. If the existing tools are useful, I prefer to connect them and build only what is missing.

## Product examples

### Operational overview

A single view for cash position, receivables and current activity.

### Conversational data capture

Transactions and documents can be registered from a conversation, reducing the need for repetitive manual entry.

### Manual entry

Structured forms are still available when a more controlled workflow is needed.

### Customer activity

Balances, transactions and pending payments can stay linked to each customer.

### Reporting

Operational data can be turned into reports that make the current state of the business easier to understand.
